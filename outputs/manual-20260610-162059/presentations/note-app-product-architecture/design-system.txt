## Design System

Slide size: 1280 x 720 (16:9)

### Typography
- Font family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif
- Headings: 600 weight, 24-40px range
  - Cover title: 40px, weight 700
  - Section header: 32px, weight 600
  - Slide title: 28px, weight 600
  - Subtitle: 20px, weight 500
- Body: 14-18px, weight 400
- Labels/Captions: 11-12px, weight 400
- Source/Copyright: 9px, weight 400

### Color Palette
- Background: #F5F5F7 (light warm gray) — matches the app's own background
- Surface: #FFFFFF (card/surface)
- Accent: #0071E3 (blue, matches app's accent color)
- Accent Dark: #0060C4
- Text Primary: #1D1D1F
- Text Secondary: #6E6E73
- Border: #D1D1D6
- Success: #1A8C3A (green)
- Warning: #E74C3C (red, used for delete)

### Spacing
- Slide padding: 48px top, 48px sides, 36px bottom
- Content gap: 16px
- Section spacing: 24px

### Layout Families
1. Cover: centered vertical, large title + subtitle
2. Split: left text (40%) + right visual/content (60%)
3. Full-bleed: content fills most of the slide
4. Matrix: 2x2 or 3x1 grid of items
5. Diagram: center architecture/flow diagram with annotations
6. Code: code block + explanation side-by-side
